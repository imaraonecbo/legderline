Privacy notice

Ledgerline collects the contact and workflow information you submit so the request can be reviewed and answered. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information through this form.

Marketing follow-up is optional and requires the separate checkbox. Submitting a service request does not automatically subscribe you to marketing. Requests may be retained in the service database until the operator configures a retention and deletion policy.

When checkout is enabled, payment details are handled by the payment provider; Ledgerline does not store card numbers. Payment confirmation is based on a verified provider webhook, not the browser return page.

Before public launch, the operator must add a verified business contact, retention period, data controller details, and a process for access/deletion requests appropriate to the jurisdictions served.
